# Subtitle picker compatibility evidence

Picker v0 owns provider absence, visible listing rows, warnings and failures, selected downloads, cue-independent
click routing, panel capture, scroll gating, and hover suppression. Provider worker generations, lane capacity,
episode retirement, geometry laws, and surface registration remain native.

Changing an explicit pick to background selection, letting off-panel wheel input scroll the list, or
suppressing hover outside the panel makes `subtitle-picker-interaction` fail.
