# Sidebar compatibility evidence

Sidebar v0 owns opening around the active cue without pausing, manual-scroll hold and deadline return, and
cue-click seeking without changing playback. Row derivation, history/mining storage, analysis publication,
surface routing, and bookmark composition remain native.

Opening at the active row instead of centering it, dropping the expiry follow, or seeking to a cue's end
makes `sidebar-navigation-interaction` fail.
